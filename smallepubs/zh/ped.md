前言 看到这部作品的重要性和价值后，不免觉得有些难以置信：当罗伯特·凯撒·柴尔德斯（ROBERT CAESAR CHILDERS）在1872年出版他的《巴利词典》第一卷时，他手中掌握的不过是几页巴利经典书籍而已。从那时起，主要得益于巴利文文本协会（Pali Text Society）的坚持不懈的努力，这些书籍几乎全部——共计约一万至一万二千页——已可供学者使用。这些书籍没有明确的作者。它们是逐渐在社区中形成的选集。就律藏和四部尼柯耶（可能附录除外）的编纂而言，它们在佛陀逝世后约一个世纪内完成；其余部分则属于随后的一个世纪。

[^2]: 原文（English）：Foreword It is somewhat hard to realize, seeing how important and valuable the work has been, that when ROBERT CAESAR CHILDERS published, in 1872, the first volume of his Pali Dictionary , he only had at his command a few pages of the canonical Pali books. Since then, owing mainly to the persistent labours of the Pali Text Society, practically the whole of these books, amounting to between ten and twelve thousand pages, have been made available to scholars. These books had no authors. They are anthologies which gradually grew up in the community. Their composition, as to the Vinaya and the four Nikāyas (with the possible exception of the supplements) was complete within about a century of the Buddha’s death; and the rest belong to the following century.

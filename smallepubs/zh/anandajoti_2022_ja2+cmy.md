## 前文（机器翻译）

Ja 2 Vaṇṇupathajātaka 关于沙地的故事 (版本 1.2) 注：这第二个本生故事对学习者有很大的兴趣，并且再次展示了一些异常现象。部分兴趣在于释义中引用经文的部分。在这里我们看到一个常见且经常使用的段落的诗化形式，这几乎接近经典内容。故事中的问题是它几乎不符合所要灌输的道德。佛陀对一位已经放弃努力的僧人说话，并告诉他自己前世的坚持。然而，故事并没有强调他的努力，本来很容易强调，只是叙述了，当有人询问时，他进入井中，打碎了一块释放出洪流的石头。故事的叙述方式似乎显示他付出了某种努力——但并不大——就完成了这一壮举。

[^2]: 原文（English）：Ja 2 Vaṇṇupathajātaka The Story about a Sandy Place (Ver 1.2) Note This second Jātaka has lots of interest for the student, and again displays something on an anomaly. Part of the interest is again in the quotations in the word commentary from the discourses. Here we see a versification of a popular and often used passage, which then borders on being canonical. The problem in the story is that it hardly fits the moral being inculcated. The Buddha speaks to a monk who has given up his efforts, and tells him of his perseverance in a previous life. But the story, rather than emphasising his effort, which it easily could have done, simply relates that, when asked, he descended into a well, and broke a stone which released a torrent of water. The way the story is told it seems to have taken some – but no great – effort to achieve.

前言 卡尔玛巴，奥金·特林雷·多杰 是扎瓦·阿底峡将这些心智训练修持从印度带到西藏的。起初，它们是秘密的，只教授给极少数人。它们被视为高级修行，仅适合有经验的修行者，因为不是每个人都能修持。它们需要巨大的勇气、决心和奉献精神。朗日唐巴大师的《心智训练八句》可能是最早向大众公开的心智训练修持。著名的噶当派上师却卡瓦大师，他创作了著名的《七点心智训练》，最初是在听到朗日唐巴的《心智训练八句》后开始跟随噶当派教法的。这些教法通过噶玛巴传统，由岗巴巴的努力得以保存下来。

[^2]: 原文（English）：Preface The Karmapa, Ogyen Trinley Dorje It was Jowo Atisha who brought these mind training practices from India to Tibet. Initially, they were secret and taught to very few people. They were regarded as a high-level practice for experienced practitioners, as not everyone was capable of practicing them. They demanded great courage, determination, and dedication. Geshe Langri Thangpa’s Eight Verses of Training the Mind was probably the first mind training practice that was opened more widely to the public. The renowned Kadampa master, Geshe Chekawa, who composed the famous Seven-Point Mind Training, first began to follow the Kadampa teachings having heard Langri Thangpa’s Eight Verses. These teachings have been preserved in the Kagyu tradition through the work of Lord Gampopa.

## 前文（机器翻译）

生命的意义在于履行责任和做一个有担当的人。许多人问我：“生命的本质是什么？生命的意义是什么？生命的价值在哪里？生命的目的是什么？”生命的意义在于履行责任和做一个有担当的人。在人生的旅程中——从出生到年老再到死亡——每个人都扮演着不同的角色。例如，孩子长大后成为父母，学生成长为教师。你可能是一个主管、一个员工，也可能是一个朋友。所有这些都是责任——人们之间共享的道德关系。当责任没有履行时，人们会说角色“非鱼非鸟”。这是用来形容奇怪现象的一个术语。从道德的角度审视自己时，我们常常会发现自己是“非鱼非鸟”。

[^2]: 原文（English）：The Meaning of Life is to Fulfill One's Duties and be Responsible Many people ask me, "What is the intrinsic quality of life? What is the meaning of life? Where is the value in life? What is the purpose of life?" The meaning of life is to fulfill one's duties and be responsible. In the journey of life — from birth, to old age, to death — every person plays different roles. For example, children grow up to be parents and students become teachers. You may be a supervisor, an employee as well as a friend. All of these are duties — ethical relationships shared between people. When duties are not fulfilled, it is said that the roles are "neither fish nor fowl". This is a term used to describe strange phenomena. When looking at ourselves from an ethical perspective, we often discover that we are "neither fish nor fowl".

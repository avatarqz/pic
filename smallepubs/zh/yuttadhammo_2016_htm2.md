## 前文（机器翻译）

将不会受到任何评判；人们只是单纯地体验每一个现象本来的样子。一开始，就像任何形式的训练一样，真正达到这种客观性可能是困难的。然而，最终，如果一个人有耐心并且坚持不懈（或者有一位也如此的老师），他将能够熟练地从一个瞬间到下一个瞬间创造清晰的觉知。这反过来会导致心灵的纯净，不受任何形式的贪婪、愤怒、恐惧、焦虑、傲慢、自负等影响。有人可能会认为，通过几乎任何类型的冥想都可以达到这种心理纯净；专注于一个概念——例如一种颜色——可以创造与专注于体验现实相同类型的客观觉知，甚至可能更容易，因为概念相对于现实更为稳定。

In the beginning, like any form of training, it may be difficult to actually attain this sort of objectivity. Eventually, though, if one is patient and persistent (or has a teacher who is so), one will become proficient in creating clear awareness from one moment to the next. This in turn will lead to purity of mind free from all forms of greed, anger, fear, anxiety, arrogance, conceit, and so on. It may be argued that this level of mental purity can be attained through almost any type of meditation; focussing on a concept—a colour, for example—can create the same sort of objective awareness as focussing on experiential reality, perhaps even more easily, given the relative stability of concepts over reality.

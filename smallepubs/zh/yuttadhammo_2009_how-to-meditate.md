## 前文（机器翻译）

简介 本小册子取自YouTube上的一个六部分视频系列（http://www.youtube.com/yuttadhammo）。它最初是为洛杉矶联邦拘留中心使用而制作的，当时无法通过视频分发教导内容，但此后它已成为我向初学者介绍冥想练习的首选方式。虽然视频提供了有用的视觉指导，但本小册子包含了许多在视频中没有的更新和扩展信息。课程安排是按照我预期新手逐步学习冥想的顺序来布局的。可能会觉得奇怪的是，第二、第三和第五章的呈现顺序与实际练习的顺序相反。其理由是，坐禅对于初学者来说最容易理解。

It was originally intended for use in the Los Angeles Federal Detention Center, where it was impossible to distribute teachings by video, but has since become my preferred means of introducing the meditation practice to newcomers in general. While the videos provide a useful visual guide, this booklet contains much updated and expanded information that is not in the videos. The lessons are laid out according to how I would expect a new-comer to learn meditation step-by-step. It may seem odd that chapters two, three, and five are presented in the opposite order in which they are to be practiced. The reasoning is that sitting meditation is easiest for a beginner to appreciate.

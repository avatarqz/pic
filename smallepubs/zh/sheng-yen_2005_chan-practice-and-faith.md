禅修与信仰 对禅修感兴趣的人往往发现很难拥有宗教信仰。由于信仰本质上是情感性的，而禅修者强调通过个人修行来获得身心的益处或体验禅，因此他们很难接受宗教信仰。这实际上是一个很大的错误。许多人认为禅修完全依赖自己的努力，需要自力更生，而念佛修行的人则完全依赖外在的帮助。这两种看法都是不正确的。实际上，禅修也需要外在的帮助，而念佛的修行同样需要自身的努力。仅凭自己的努力，很难成为一个有成就的禅修者。

[^2]: 原文（English）：Chan Practice and Faith People interested in Chan practice often find it difficult to have religious faith. As faith is intrinsically emotional, and Chan practitioners emphasize personal cultivation to gain physical and mental benefits or the experience of Chan, they find it hard to accept religious faith. This is actually a great mistake. Many people think that Chan practice depends solely on their own efforts, requiring self-reliance, while those who practice by reciting the Buddha's name depend solely on external help. Both of these views are incorrect. In reality, Chan practice also requires external help, and the practice of reciting the Buddha's name also requires one's own effort. One can hardly become an accomplished Chan practitioner through one's own efforts.

宗喀巴·洛藏达巴敬颂。你的形态如初晨闪耀的金色云彩般美丽，你的言语如不朽尊者那悦耳的声音般甜美，你的心智如大海那无尽的液体宝藏般深邃——胜利者之父文殊，我向你顶礼。向你致敬，因你在无数时期、无数世界中，无边无际地精进倾听无数胜利者的教诲，最终成为无上智慧之主，我向你顶礼。向你致敬，因你在轮回长存之际，将持续守护所有疲惫奔波的众生，无可匹敌的慈悲之主，无畏承受众生沉重之负担，我向你顶礼。通过在此倾注哪怕微不足道的一滴水，记录由诸界第一大菩萨为数量如虚空般的众生所行的不可思议功德之海，愿尊贵的文殊常为引导者，在每一生中展示无上的乘道，并愿他解救沉浸于苦难之海的善良众生，使所有众生皆能像文殊自身般殊胜。

Your form is as beautiful as the glowing golden clouds of a fresh dawn, Your speech as sweet as the melodious voice of the immortal lord,1 Your mind as deep as the great liquid treasury of the sea — Mañjughoṣa, father of the victorious, I bow to you in homage. To you, who for infinite aeons in infinite realms Strove boundlessly to listen to countless teachings From countless victorious ones, ultimately to become The lord of incomparable knowledge, I bow. To you, who for as long as saṃsāra endures Will remain to safeguard all careworn beings, Peerless lord of compassion, undaunted By the great burdens of sentient beings, I bow. Through setting down here, however inadequately, A mere droplet from the ocean of wondrous deeds Performed by the foremost bodhisattva in all realms For the sake of beings whose number is as vast as space, May the venerable Mañjughoṣa always be a guide Revealing the supreme vehicle in every lifetime, and May he free the kind ones immersed in misery’s ocean So that all may come to resemble Mañjughoṣa himself. | Translated by Adam Pearcey, 2021.

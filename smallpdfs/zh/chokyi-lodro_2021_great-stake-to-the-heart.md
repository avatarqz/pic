This one who was born at the end of the degenerate age Is beset by mental afflictions, mind’s five poisons. He has received profound and vast Dharma instruction, But left everything on paper or as dry and empty words. The real meaning has not seeped into his heart. His stubborn temperament’s impossible to tame, Mind’s nature cloaked in the darkness of thought, Besieged by the brigands of dullness and agitation.

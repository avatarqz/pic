# 源语种=pi 目标语种=lzh 句数 33->31 ratio=1.226

── 段落 1 ──
  SRC │ 1. Dhammacakkappavattanasuttaṃ 1081. Ekaṃ samayaṃ bhagavā bārāṇasiyaṃ viharati isipatane migadāye
  TGT │ (无对应)

── 段落 2 ──
  SRC │ Tatra kho bhagavā pañcavaggiye bhikkhū āmantesi – ‘‘dveme, bhikkhave, antā pabbajitena na sevitabbā
  SRC │ Katame dve? Yo cāyaṃ kāmesu kāmasukhallikānuyogo hīno gammo pothujjaniko anariyo anatthasaṃhito, yo cāyaṃ attakilamathānuyogo dukkho anariyo anatthasaṃhito
  TGT │ 聞如是：一時，佛在波羅柰國鹿野樹下坐，時有千比丘諸天神，皆大會側塞空中，於是有自然法輪，飛來當佛前轉。

── 段落 3 ──
  SRC │ Ete kho, bhikkhave, ubho ante anupagamma majjhimā paṭipadā tathāgatena abhisambuddhā cakkhukaraṇī ñāṇakaraṇī upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati’’. ‘‘Katamā ca sā, bhikkhave, majjhimā paṭipadā tathāgatena abhisambuddhā cakkhukaraṇī ñāṇakaraṇī upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati? Ayameva ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo, seyyathidaṃ – sammādiṭṭhi sammāsaṅkappo sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvo sammāvāyāmo sammāsati sammāsamādhi
  TGT │ 佛以手撫輪曰：「止！
  TGT │ 往者吾從無數劫來，為名色轉受苦無量，今者癡愛之意已止，漏結之情已解，諸根已定生死已斷，不復轉於五道也。

── 段落 4 ──
  SRC │ Ayaṃ kho sā, bhikkhave, majjhimā paṭipadā tathāgatena abhisambuddhā cakkhukaraṇī ñāṇakaraṇī upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati
  TGT │ (无对应)

── 段落 5 ──
  SRC │ ‘‘Idaṃ kho pana, bhikkhave, dukkhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ – jātipi dukkhā, jarāpi dukkhā, byādhipi dukkho, maraṇampi dukkhaṃ, appiyehi sampayogo dukkho, piyehi vippayogo dukkho, yampicchaṃ na labhati tampi dukkhaṃ – saṃkhittena pañcupādānakkhandhā dukkhā
  TGT │ 」輪即止。
  TGT │ 於是，佛告諸比丘：「世間有二事墮邊行，行道弟子捨家者，終身不當與從事。

── 段落 6 ──
  SRC │ Idaṃ kho pana, bhikkhave, dukkhasamudayaṃ ariyasaccaṃ – yāyaṃ taṇhā ponobbhavikā nandirāgasahagatā tatratatrābhinandinī, seyyathidaṃ – kāmataṇhā, bhavataṇhā, vibhavataṇhā
  TGT │ 何等二？
  TGT │ 一為念在貪欲無清淨志，二為猗著身愛不能精進。

── 段落 7 ──
  SRC │ (无对应)
  TGT │ 是故退邊行，不得值佛道德具人。

── 段落 8 ──
  SRC │ Idaṃ kho pana, bhikkhave, dukkhanirodhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ – yo tassāyeva taṇhāya asesavirāganirodho cāgo paṭinissaggo mutti anālayo
  SRC │ Idaṃ kho pana, bhikkhave, dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā ariyasaccaṃ – ayameva ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo, seyyathidaṃ – sammādiṭṭhi sammāsamādhi
  TGT │ 若此比丘不念貪欲著身愛行，可得受中，如來最正覺得眼得慧，從兩邊度自致泥洹。
  TGT │ 何謂受中？

── 段落 9 ──
  SRC │ ‘‘‘Idaṃ dukkhaṃ ariyasacca’nti me, bhikkhave, pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu cakkhuṃ udapādi, ñāṇaṃ udapādi, paññā udapādi, vijjā udapādi, āloko udapādi
  SRC │ ‘Taṃ kho panidaṃ dukkhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ pariññeyya’nti me, bhikkhave, pubbe udapādi
  TGT │ 謂受八直之道，一曰正見，二曰正思，三曰正言，四曰正行，五曰正命，六曰正治，七曰正志，八曰正定。

── 段落 10 ──
  SRC │ (无对应)
  TGT │ 若諸比丘本末聞道，當已知甚苦為真諦，已一心受眼受禪思受慧見，覺所念令意解，當知甚苦習盡為真諦，已受眼觀禪思慧見，覺所念令意解，如是盡真諦。

── 段落 11 ──
  SRC │ ‘Taṃ kho panidaṃ dukkhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ pariññāta’nti me, bhikkhave, pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu cakkhuṃ udapādi, ñāṇaṃ udapādi, paññā udapādi, vijjā udapādi, āloko udapādi
  TGT │ 何謂為苦？
  TGT │ 謂生老苦、病苦，憂、悲、惱苦，怨憎會苦，所愛別苦，求不得苦，要從五陰受盛為苦。

── 段落 12 ──
  SRC │ ‘‘‘Idaṃ dukkhasamudayaṃ ariyasacca’nti me, bhikkhave, pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu cakkhuṃ udapādi, ñāṇaṃ udapādi, paññā udapādi, vijjā udapādi, āloko udapādi
  SRC │ ‘Taṃ kho panidaṃ dukkhasamudayaṃ ariyasaccaṃ pahātabba’nti me, bhikkhave, pubbe udapādi
  TGT │ 何謂苦習？
  TGT │ 謂從愛故而令復有樂性，不離在在貪憙，欲愛、色愛、不色之愛，是習為苦。

── 段落 13 ──
  SRC │ ‘Taṃ kho panidaṃ dukkhasamudayaṃ ariyasaccaṃ pahīna’nti me, bhikkhave, pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu cakkhuṃ udapādi, ñāṇaṃ udapādi, paññā udapādi, vijjā udapādi, āloko udapādi
  TGT │ 何謂苦盡？
  TGT │ 謂覺從愛復有所樂，婬念不受，不念無餘無婬，捨之無復禪，如是為習盡。

── 段落 14 ──
  SRC │ ‘‘‘Idaṃ dukkhanirodhaṃ ariyasacca’nti me, bhikkhave, pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu cakkhuṃ udapādi, ñāṇaṃ udapādi, paññā udapādi, vijjā udapādi, āloko udapādi
  SRC │ ‘Taṃ kho panidaṃ dukkhanirodhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ sacchikātabba’nti me, bhikkhave, pubbe udapādi
  TGT │ 何謂苦習盡欲受道？
  TGT │ 謂受行八直道，正見、正思、正言、正行、正命、正治、正志、正定，是為苦習盡受道真諦也。

── 段落 15 ──
  SRC │ ‘Taṃ kho panidaṃ dukkhanirodhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ sacchikata’nti me, bhikkhave, pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu cakkhuṃ udapādi, ñāṇaṃ udapādi, paññā udapādi, vijjā udapādi, āloko udapādi
  SRC │ ‘‘‘Idaṃ dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā ariyasacca’nti me, bhikkhave, pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu cakkhuṃ udapādi, ñāṇaṃ udapādi, paññā udapādi, vijjā udapādi, āloko udapādi
  TGT │ 又是，比丘！
  TGT │ 苦為真諦，苦由習為真諦，苦習盡為真諦，苦習盡欲受道為真諦，若本在昔未聞是法者，當受眼觀禪行受慧見受覺念令意得解。

── 段落 16 ──
  SRC │ Taṃ kho panidaṃ dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā ariyasaccaṃ bhāvetabba’nti me, bhikkhave, pubbe udapādi
  TGT │ (无对应)

── 段落 17 ──
  SRC │ ‘Taṃ kho panidaṃ dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā ariyasaccaṃ bhāvita’nti me, bhikkhave, pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu cakkhuṃ udapādi, ñāṇaṃ udapādi, paññā udapādi, vijjā udapādi, āloko udapādi
  TGT │ 若令在斯未聞是四諦法者，當受道眼、受禪思、受慧覺，令意行解。

── 段落 18 ──
  SRC │ ‘‘Yāvakīvañca me, bhikkhave, imesu catūsu ariyasaccesu evaṃ tiparivaṭṭaṃ dvādasākāraṃ yathābhūtaṃ ñāṇadassanaṃ na suvisuddhaṃ ahosi, neva tāvāhaṃ, bhikkhave, sadevake loke samārake sabrahmake sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiyā pajāya sadevamanussāya ‘anuttaraṃ sammāsambodhiṃ abhisambuddho’ti paccaññāsiṃ . ‘‘Yato ca kho me, bhikkhave, imesu catūsu ariyasaccesu evaṃ tiparivaṭṭaṃ dvādasākāraṃ yathābhūtaṃ ñāṇadassanaṃ suvisuddhaṃ ahosi, athāhaṃ, bhikkhave, sadevake loke samārake sabrahmake sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiyā pajāya sadevamanussāya ‘anuttaraṃ sammāsambodhiṃ abhisambuddho’ti paccaññāsiṃ
  TGT │ 若諸在彼不得聞是四諦法者，亦當受眼、受禪、受慧、受覺，令意得解，是為四諦三轉合十二事，知而未淨者吾不與也。
  TGT │ 一切世間諸天人民，若梵、若魔、沙門、梵志，自知證已，受行戒、定、慧、解、度知見成，是為四極。

── 段落 19 ──
  SRC │ Ñāṇañca pana me dassanaṃ udapādi – ‘akuppā me vimutti , ayamantimā jāti, natthidāni punabbhavo’’’ti
  SRC │ Idamavoca bhagavā
  TGT │ 是生後不復有，長離世間無復憂患。

── 段落 20 ──
  SRC │ Attamanā pañcavaggiyā bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṃ abhinandunti
  SRC │ Imasmiñca pana veyyākaraṇasmiṃ bhaññamāne āyasmato koṇḍaññassa virajaṃ vītamalaṃ dhammacakkhuṃ udapādi – ‘‘yaṃ kiñci samudayadhammaṃ, sabbaṃ taṃ nirodhadhamma’’nti
  TGT │ 」佛說是時，賢者阿若拘鄰等及八千姟天，皆遠塵離垢諸法眼生。

── 段落 21 ──
  SRC │ Pavattite ca pana bhagavatā dhammacakke bhummā devā saddamanussāvesuṃ – ‘‘etaṃ bhagavatā bārāṇasiyaṃ isipatane migadāye anuttaraṃ dhammacakkaṃ pavattitaṃ appaṭivattiyaṃ samaṇena vā brāhmaṇena vā devena vā mārena vā brahmunā vā kenaci vā lokasmi’’nti
  SRC │ Bhummānaṃ devānaṃ saddaṃ sutvā cātumahārājikā devā saddamanussāvesuṃ – ‘‘etaṃ bhagavatā bārāṇasiyaṃ isipatane migadāye anuttaraṃ dhammacakkaṃ pavattitaṃ, appaṭivattiyaṃ samaṇena vā brāhmaṇena vā devena vā mārena vā brahmunā vā kenaci vā lokasmi’’nti
  TGT │ 其千比丘漏盡意解，皆得阿羅漢，及上諸習法應當盡者一切皆轉。
  TGT │ 眾祐法輪聲三轉，諸天世間在法地者莫不遍聞，至于第一四天王、忉利天、焰天、兜術天、不驕樂天、化應聲天，至諸梵界須臾遍聞。

── 段落 22 ──
  SRC │ Cātumahārājikānaṃ devānaṃ saddaṃ sutvā tāvatiṃsā devā yāmā devā tusitā devā nimmānaratī devā paranimmitavasavattī devā brahmakāyikā devā saddamanussāvesuṃ – ‘‘etaṃ bhagavatā bārāṇasiyaṃ isipatane migadāye anuttaraṃ dhammacakkaṃ pavattitaṃ appaṭivattiyaṃ samaṇena vā brāhmaṇena vā devena vā mārena vā brahmunā vā kenaci vā lokasmi’’nti
  TGT │ 爾時，佛界三千日月萬二千天地皆大震動，是為佛眾祐，始於波羅柰以無上法輪轉未轉者，照無數度諸天人從是得道。
